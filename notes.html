Wave Motion

Simple explanations for Class 11–12 Physics.

01

What is a Wave?

Transfer of energy through a disturbance

📖 Simple Meaning

A wave is a disturbance that transfers energy from one place to another without transporting matter as a whole.

💡 Remember

Waves transfer energy, not matter.

🌍 Examples

  • 🌊 Water waves
  • 🔊 Sound waves
  • 💡 Light waves
  • 📡 Radio waves
02

Types of Waves

Based on the direction of particle vibration

∿∿∿

Transverse Wave

Particles vibrate perpendicular to the direction of wave propagation.

Example: Light waves
⇆ ⇆ ⇆

Longitudinal Wave

Particles vibrate parallel to the direction of wave propagation.

Example: Sound waves
03

Important Wave Parameters

Quantities used to describe a wave

A

Amplitude

Maximum displacement from mean position.

λ

Wavelength

Distance between two consecutive crests.

f

Frequency

Number of oscillations per second.

T

Time Period

Time required for one complete oscillation.

04

Important Formulas

Remember these for solving numerical problems.

v = fλ
v Wave speed (m/s)
f Frequency (Hz)
λ Wavelength (m)
⭐ Also Remember

Frequency and time period are related by:

f = 1/T

🧠 Important Exam Notes

01 Waves transfer energy.
02 Frequency is measured in Hertz.
03 Wavelength is measured in metres.
04 Amplitude determines the maximum displacement.
05 Wave speed is v = fλ.
06 Sound is a mechanical wave.
